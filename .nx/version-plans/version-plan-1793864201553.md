Added dormant Ghost content identity operations: `browseContentIdentities()`, `replaceContent()`, `deleteContent()`, and `reconcileContent()` group, replace, delete, and reconcile package-managed records by stable Ghost content ID instead of slug. The existing slug-based operations, settings behavior, and error shape are unchanged, and nothing uses the new operations until an index opts in through the one-time identity migration.
