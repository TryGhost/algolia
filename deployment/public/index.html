Ghost + Algolia

Connect your publication.

Connect Ghost to keep your Algolia index up to date when posts change.

Indexing status has not been checked. Enable JavaScript to check this deployment.

  1. 1

    Verify your existing webhook secret

    Enter the NETLIFY_KEY already saved in Netlify. This page cannot change it.

    Your secret is sent to this site to verify it, then included in the URLs you copy to Ghost. It is not saved by this page.

  2. 2

    Add four webhooks in Ghost

    Open Settings → Integrations in Ghost Admin. Create a custom integration called “Algolia search”, then add these webhooks.

    EventURL to use
    Post publishedPublish and update
    Published post editedPublish and update
    Post unpublishedUnpublish and delete
    Post deletedUnpublish and delete
  3. 3

    Check your index

    Publish a test post with a distinctive phrase and find it in your Algolia index. Edit the post to check updates, then unpublish it to check removal.

    Starting with an empty index? Import your existing posts with the CLI first. This requires a terminal. If your index already works, keep it; you do not need to reimport.
  4. 4

    Finish setup

    Once your test post appears, updates, and disappears from Algolia as expected, setup is complete. You can close this page. Your webhooks will keep running.
